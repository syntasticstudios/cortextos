
## 2026-05-07 00:28 — Eignungstest → Direkter Checkout-Pfad

**Trigger:** The eignungstest at /medizin/eignungstest is a well-built 5-step qualification quiz. But after completing it, the result likely ends with a generic CTA (e.g., "Jetzt Arzt finden") rather than a personalized recommendation. A user who just told us they have chronic pain and have tried conventional treatments is primed to convert — we're not capturing that momentum.

**Suggestion:** After step 5 of the eignungstest, show a personalized result screen: if score suggests suitability, display a direct "Jetzt Rezept beantragen" CTA that pre-fills the checkout questionnaire (Fragebogen step) with the answers already given. This reduces duplicate data entry and creates a smooth funnel: Eignungstest → Checkout, skipping the repeated questionnaire fields.

**Effort estimate:** M (checkout state management + eignungstest result routing)
**Impact:** L — converts qualified prospects directly instead of dropping them back at the homepage
**Reference:** Similar to flowzz.com's guided product selection that pre-fills checkout. Also common in Telemedizin portals (Doctolib, Zava) where intake questionnaires feed into appointment booking.
**Open question for user:** Should eignungstest answers persist in localStorage so the checkout Fragebogen step can auto-fill them? Or would you prefer a "continue as" approach where the result links to the checkout with URL params?

## 2026-05-07 02:32 — Role-aware registration funnel: "Als Patient / Arzt / Apotheke registrieren"

**Trigger:** /registrieren presents a single generic registration form. Doctors and pharmacies who land on this page have no way to self-identify their role — the current flow likely requires admin-side role assignment after registration, creating friction and support burden. New doctors and pharmacies lack a clear, trusted entry point.

**Suggestion:** Add a role-selection step at the start of /registrieren — a simple three-card picker ("Als Patient registrieren", "Als Arzt registrieren", "Als Apotheke registrieren") that routes to role-specific registration flows. Doctor/pharmacy flows could collect additional professional fields (Approbationsnummer, BSNR, etc.) inline and set Clerk user metadata before Clerk sign-up, enabling automatic role provisioning without admin intervention.

**Effort estimate:** M (role-picker screen is trivial; depth is in doctor/pharmacy field collection and Clerk metadata write)

**Impact:** L — reduces support overhead for manual role assignment, increases trust for professional users landing on a generic consumer form, enables automatic onboarding routing

**Reference:** DoctoLib registration separates patient vs practitioner from the first CTA. Flowzz.com has separate partner signup path. Industry standard for two-sided health platforms.

**Open question for user:** Should doctors/pharmacies be gated behind an invitation/verification step before completing registration, or self-registration open with verification review after?

## 2026-05-07 10:55 — Price-Alert / Preisalarm für Produkte

**Trigger:** While hunting the catalog and Preisverlauf section, I noticed the chart exists but is empty — yet the underlying use case is strong: patients on fixed budgets check back repeatedly to see if a product got cheaper.

**Suggestion:** "Preisalarm" feature — patient can set a price threshold on any product detail page ("Benachrichtige mich, wenn Preis unter X,XX €"). When any pharmacy's offer drops below the threshold, send a Telegram/email notification. Uses existing offer data already synced from HiGreen/Cannaleo.

**Effort estimate:** M (backend: threshold table + cron check against latest offers; frontend: threshold input on product detail; infra: existing Telegram bus already in place)
**Impact:** L — directly drives repeat engagement and purchase conversion. Patients already compare prices manually; automating this saves them daily check-ins.
**Reference:** Amazon price tracker / camelcamelcamel for physical products; flowzz.com has no price alert — first-mover advantage.
**Open question for user:** Should alerts be per-product (any pharmacy hits threshold) or per-product+pharmacy combination? And should there be a max frequency limit (e.g. once per 24h)?
