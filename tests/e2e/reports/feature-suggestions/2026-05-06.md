## Feature: Pharmacy Card "Live Products" Badge
**Hunt cycle**: target=6 (pharmacy finder), 2026-05-06

**Observation**: Pharmacy cards show name, address, delivery options, and provider badge — but no indication of how many products are available at that pharmacy. Patients must click through to the pharmacy detail and then navigate to products to check availability.

**Proposal**: Add a small "X Produkte verfügbar" badge on each pharmacy card in the finder list. Data source: count of offers with `availability=true` from the pharmacy's offers. Updated on the daily sync cycle.

**Why it matters**: 
- Reduces dead-end clicks for patients seeking a pharmacy with their preferred product
- Differentiates high-assortment pharmacies from low-assortment ones at a glance
- Leverages Cannametrics data already being collected (availability feeds)
- Creates a natural quality signal: pharmacies with more listed products get more clicks

**Effort**: S (add offer count to pharmacy query, render badge on card)
**Impact**: L (reduces friction in pharmacy discovery, boosts Cannametrics data utilization)
**Anti-pattern addressed**: #6 — navigating blind without pre-filtering context
